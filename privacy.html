Privacy

What this website collects, and where your request goes.

The request form

The form asks only for what LUGOD needs to reply: the kind of event, date, area, guest count, your name and mobile number, plus anything optional you choose to add. Please don't include ID or payment details.

Nothing is sent until you check your summary and choose how to send it. If you send by email, Messenger, Viber or text, your request travels through that app to LUGOD. If LUGOD connects a form service, your request goes to LUGOD's inbox through that service.

Saved in your browser

While you type, the form keeps a draft in your own browser so you don't lose it. The draft is cleared when you finish. Nobody else can see it. You can also clear it by clearing your browser's site data.

Maps and fonts

The map on the About page only loads from Google when you select "Show the map". Fonts load from Google Fonts. This site doesn't use advertising trackers.

Questions

Email lugodcateringservices@gmail.com to ask LUGOD about information you've sent them.
