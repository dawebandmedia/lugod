Lugod means delight

LUGOD Catering & Events Management has been cooking and styling celebrations from Novaliches, Quezon City since 2023. The name says what the team is after: taste the joy, share the moment.

Food and styling from one team

LUGOD cooks the buffet, builds the set-up and sends people to run the day: a coordinator with the programme, and a service team at the buffet and the food carts.

Their work runs from kiddie parties with lighted letters and balloon arches, to baptisms, debuts and anniversaries, to company townhalls. On quieter days the kitchen packs party trays and bento meals.
